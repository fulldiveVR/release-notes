---

- Sign-in now uses a single Roomcord entry screen with email-first routing. Returning users are directed to the appropriate workspace, server, or server list, while sign-out and forced logout return to a clean start.
- Workspace servers can be created, joined, switched, branded, and deleted by authorized owners. Server-specific rooms, drafts, sessions, notifications, and live connections stay separated.
- Room apps now support interactive custom screens, site apps, richer chat cards, file and image workflows, full-screen views, scheduled actions, and automatic renewal of expiring app sessions.
- Calls and realtime updates are more reliable when switching servers, with improved device selection on desktop and smoother video background effects.
- Authentication and account flows now support optional phone verification, clearer email sign-in details, improved invite handling, and complete account deletion across all workspaces.
