- Added built-in room apps, including Board, To-do, Dashboard, and a CEO overview for eligible Shadow rooms, with live updates and app installation support.
- Added room-authored app support with generic app screens, forms, drafts, approval workflows, and clearer labels.
- Added a Files tab with folders, all-files browsing, trash, and improved image handling.
- Improved collaboration with multiple room owners, replies to specific posts, complete thread pagination, and links between call reports and Board cards.
- Improved interactive cards and calls with pending and cancel states, disabled actions after failed checks, clearer call progress, and persistent assistant work after voice calls.
- Improved reliability and performance across deep links, room navigation, search controls, message copying, image rendering, and desktop window restoration.
