- Calls now ring more consistently across mobile, desktop, and web, with improved device handoff, picture-in-picture behavior, missed-call handling, and call state synchronization.
- Rooms open at the first unread message with a clear new-message marker, and read status updates more accurately.
- Room dashboards can open registered actions through the app, and dashboard downloads and embedded web content are more reliable.
- External room agents are better supported, with clearer agent identity, thread-scoped replies, asynchronous responses, and improved reply testing.
- Added a configurable Center web app entry point and improved localization, including more complete call-related permission prompts.
