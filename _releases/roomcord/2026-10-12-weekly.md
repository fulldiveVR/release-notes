- Meeting calls now show clear per-meeting agent names, preserve conversation context, and improve voice-agent routing when people join, interrupt, or redirect requests.
- Calls are more reliable across mobile, desktop, and multiple devices, with improved ringing, handoff, picture-in-picture behavior, missed-call handling, and call cleanup.
- Agent responses support richer interactive content, including charts, diagrams, metrics, tables, code, timelines, and kanban boards, with actions and downloads where available.
- Room dashboards can now handle interactive actions and provide downloadable HTML content; a configurable center app is also available from navigation.
- Rooms open at the first unread message with a clear new-message marker, while message loading and scrolling remain more consistent.
- Marketplace listings now show ratings and reviews, support direct chats from more agent cards, and provide clearer feedback when reviews cannot be saved.
