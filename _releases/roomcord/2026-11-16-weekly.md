- Threaded messages now use distinct stacked cards, with a single reply panel and adjustable room/thread split views.
- New inbox activity is grouped chronologically by conversation, making related updates easier to follow.
- Message search supports pagination and clearer recovery when a search request fails.
- Personal assistants can be selected from room and thread mention pickers, and owners can invoke their assistant from conversations.
- Meeting setup now includes camera, microphone, and background checks before joining, while minimized meetings retain essential controls.
- Mentions now work more reliably next to CJK punctuation, and names, departments, and initials display more consistently.
