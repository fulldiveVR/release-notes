- Edit received images with drawing, shapes, arrows, text, cropping, undo, and clear tools, then attach the marked-up result to a room or thread.
- SVG images are identified as unsupported for editing, with localized guidance and clearer image-editing controls.
- Voice agents remain in listening mode after joining and respond more reliably when users interrupt or speak over them.
- Unread badges and read status are more accurate, including messages and agent replies viewed in an open chat.
- Scheduled agent results are delivered more reliably to their intended conversations, with clearer next-run information.
- Image saves now use the device gallery on mobile, while desktop navigation, footers, and room visibility behave more consistently.
