summary: This release adds catalog-driven A2UI capabilities, scheduled action handlers, and improvements to reliability, recovery, and upgrade operations.
---

- A2UI catalogs can now be provided by packaged skills, with catalog-based validation and support for user actions that update the same surface without creating a new one.
- Schedules can target published action handlers instead of prompts, with configurable inputs and timeouts. Action executions recover safely across restarts and do not unnecessarily wake the runtime.
- Tenants remain active while detached background jobs are running, then return to idle hibernation after those jobs finish.
- Codex upstream requests retry selected transient failures before falling back, improving resilience during temporary service interruptions.
- Read-heavy control-plane operations use a dedicated read pool, reducing contention with writes and improving responsiveness during high activity.
- Hub upgrades and restarts preserve active connections and durable work more reliably through process handoff and improved recovery behavior.
