- Structured A2A and A2UI inputs and outputs are now preserved, validated, and delivered more reliably, including durable steering, progress labels, and equivalent retries.
- Browser automation recovers more reliably after page timeouts, with an updated browser stack and improved support for international punctuation and file delivery.
- Scheduled executions use isolated lanes, while worker-pool reconciliation and shutdown handling improve operational stability.
- Signed tenant file downloads now avoid stale caching, ensuring overwritten content is served correctly.
- Provider-bound reasoning recovery and stale-run cleanup improvements increase resilience during long-running or interrupted work.
